The ring spoon, kept.

04 · Covers

No photos? No problem.

Most imported recipes won't have a good photo. Every recipe gets a cover instead: a tinted tile with one word from its title in italic serif, toned by its main tag. When a photo exists, it simply replaces the cover. Grids look intentional either way.

05 · Principles

Simple, and it feels like an app.

One tomato button

Each screen has one primary action, in tomato, in the thumb zone. Everything else is quieter.

Sheets, not pages

Pick a day, save to a household, sort a list: these open bottom sheets over the current screen, so you never lose your place.

Undo, not “are you sure?”

Delete, tick, have-it and remove all act instantly with an Undo toast. No confirmation dialogs.

Households are tabs

Switching between your recipes and a shared household is one tap on a binder divider, never a menu.

Press, don't hover

Everything scales slightly on press. 44px minimum targets. Nothing important hides behind hover.

Calm by default

Show the next useful thing, fold the rest away: bought items collapse, past days fade, extra fields wait in “Review details”.
